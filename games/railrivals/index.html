How to play

  • Goal: score the most points by claiming routes, finishing your tickets and building the longest line.
  • On your turn do one thing:
    • Take 2 cards from the station row or the deck. A face-up wild 🌈 counts as both cards, and can't be your second pick.
    • Claim a route: tap it and pay as many cards of its color as it has cars. Grey routes take any one color. Wild cards replace any color.
    • Draw tickets: look at 3 and keep at least 1.
    • Build a station: tap an empty city. The 1st costs 1 card, the 2nd 2 cards of one color, the 3rd 3 cards of one color.
  • Stations 🚉: each of your stations lets you use one rival route out of its city to finish your tickets (picked for you at the end, the best choice). Every station you never build is worth +4.
  • Tunnels ⛰️ (dashed frame): after you pay, 3 cards are turned over from the deck. Each one in your colour, and every wild, costs one more card of that colour or a wild. If you can't or don't want to pay, you take your cards back and your turn ends.
  • Ferries ⛴️ (rainbow dots): each dot must be paid with a wild card.
  • Route points: 1 car = 1, 2 = 2, 3 = 4, 4 = 7, 5 = 10, 6 = 15.
  • Tickets: connect the two cities with your own trains to add the points at the end. Unfinished tickets subtract them.
  • Double routes: with 2–3 players only one of the two tracks can be used. Nobody can own both.
  • Longest line: +10 for the longest continuous line.
  • The end: when someone has 2 or fewer trains left, everyone gets one last turn. The game also ends when no one can claim any route any more. If the deck runs out, the discards are shuffled into a new one.
