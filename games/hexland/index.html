📜 HEXLAND RULES

🎯 Goal: The first player to reach 10 Victory Points (VP) wins.

👥 Play with Friends: Everyone joins the room link on their own device. You can watch the board at any time, but you can only move on your turn. Your cards are only visible to you, in your hand at the bottom.

🏦 Bank: The bank holds 19 cards of each resource. Paid and discarded cards go back to it. If it can't pay everyone a resource, nobody gets that resource.

🏠 Points: Settlement = 1 VP, City = 2 VP, Longest Road (min 5) = 2 VP, Largest Army (min 3 knights) = 2 VP, Victory Point Card = 1 VP.

🎲 Dice & Harvest: When the dice total matches a tile's number, owners of adjacent settlements/cities collect resources (cities give 2x).

🦹 Rolling a 7: Everyone holding more than 7 cards discards half. The current player moves the robber and steals 1 card from a neighbor.
